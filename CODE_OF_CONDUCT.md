# Code of conduct

Arena contributors and maintainers must treat one another with respect in issues,
pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Give specific feedback about the work. Accept corrections, acknowledge mistakes,
and allow others to participate. Do not harass people, use discriminatory abuse,
make threats, or share another person's private information without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation to enforce these expectations. Enforcement depends on the conduct
and its impact.

Report concerns privately to a maintainer when a private channel is available.
If you need a private channel, ask for one without posting sensitive details in
public. Do not publish private reports or reporter identities without consent.
